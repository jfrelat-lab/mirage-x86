MIRAGE serves the archive exactly as published, free of charge; nothing in it is modified.
examples/games/cdogs/SOUND.CFG is ours (MIT): what the archive's DSETUP32.EXE writes for a Sound
Blaster 16 at 220h, IRQ 7, DMA 5, 22 kHz stereo. The player puts it next to the unpacked game.

The README also says some of the sound effects were "borrowed from some other games"; they are
served as part of the author's unmodified archive.
